CONTROLS & RULES +

Move. Scroll to slide the bottom paddle left or right. You can also use ← / → or A / D, move your mouse across the court, or drag on a touchscreen. Hit off-center to aim.

Clear the wall. Forty bricks, four rows: blue, yellow, red, green. Each hit removes one brick. Clear all forty to win.

Cups. Two white cups with gold beer sit in no-man’s-land. Hit either one and you drink. The ball stops; press Space or tap to serve again. Bricks stay cleared.

Keep playing. Miss the paddle and serve again. Cup hits and misses are counted separately. Space serves or pauses; New Game resets everything.

NO QUARTERS REQUIRED.
