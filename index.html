CONTROLS & RULES +

Move. Scroll the mouse wheel, use ↑ / ↓ or W / S, or drag on the court. You play on the left. Hit off-center on your paddle to aim.

Score. During a rally, hit the computer’s cup for a point. On a serve, hitting either cup awards the receiver a point. Hit your own cup and the computer gets a point. Miss a paddle? Nobody scores; the player who missed serves again.

Viner rule. You cannot lose on an own-cup hit or a serve hitting either cup. If your opponent already has four, the point is canceled. They have to hit your cup during a rally to win. First to five.

Serve. Move to choose your serve height, then press Space or tap the court. Space also pauses a rally. Cups stop every shot, including your own.
